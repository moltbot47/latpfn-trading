Acquisition Strategies

Local Card Shops (10-30% below market)
Build relationships. Buy collections they don't want to sort. Many LCS price below TCGPlayer mid.
Garage Sales / Estate Sales (50-90% below)
EstateSales.net, Craigslist, FB Marketplace. Most sellers don't know modern values.
eBay Bulk Lots (30-70% below)
Search "pokemon card lot" sorted by newly listed + auction. Bid on unsorted estate lots.
Facebook Buy/Sell/Trade Groups (10-20% below)
Direct seller = no platform fees = lower prices. Join Pokemon TCG BST groups.
Japanese Import (30-50% cheaper)
Buy via Buyee (Yahoo Auctions JP), Mercari JP, Amazon JP. JP alt arts often much cheaper before English reprint.
PSA Grading Arbitrage (2-5x premium)
Buy raw NM at raw price, grade PSA 10, sell at 2-5x. Cost: $20-30/card. Success rate: ~30-40% grade 10.
Sealed Product Hold (10-30% annual)
Out-of-print booster boxes appreciate steadily. Key sets: Evolving Skies, VSTAR Universe (JP), Prismatic Evolutions.
Pre-Release Events (variable edge)
Attend local pre-release tournaments. Get early access before full release price discovery. Sell chase pulls immediately.
